  Buttons dim when they can't be bought right now (population full).

  popup "Build a Tower 250" with buy button ≈ (850,730).

  x 814 infantry (damage/armor) · 962 support (damage/range) · 1110 anti-armor (damage/armor) ·
  1260 armor · 1408 turrets (damage/range) · 1556 income (+10%) · 1704 building (Dino Hut 400 → unlocks unit 4).
  Rows' buy buttons at (1645,578) and (1645,780); close X at (1830,685) (two rows) or (1830,580) (one row).
